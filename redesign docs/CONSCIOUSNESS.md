# The eight consciousnesses (and a proposed ninth)

Working proposal, 20 Oct 2026. One consciousness persona per sample. Each is the person that sample would grow up to be. Agents: this is the home of the personas; the shared voice rules stay in `VOICE_GUIDE.md` and the samples themselves in `SAMPLES.md`.

## Shared rules (decisions 39 to 41, `COMBAT_MATRIX.md` 10; decision 42 is Not of This World only)

- The consciousness is a soul not yet born (not the sperm, not the swimmer's own head), and wants the egg because conception is its way into the world. It speaks mostly as "we", like a coach or a passenger (one team); as "you" when it blames or accuses; as "me" or "I" only rarely, when it is being nakedly selfish (user ruling, 20 Oct; `VOICE_GUIDE.md` 2).
- **Two exceptions to "we":** Alpha (the self-help coach) and Prawn Again (who remembers earlier runs: "you did this last time") may speak to the swimmer as "you" freely; it is how their personas work (user ruling, 20 Oct).
- **Every consciousness line is written per sample**, shared Feat gift lines included (`FEATS.md` 1). There is no generic consciousness line.
- **A new soul every run.** 1A and 1B are different souls of the same temperament. **Prawn Again is the exception:** it remembers earlier runs.
- **It doesn't know the lab exists.** It believes it is exactly where it is supposed to be. No Scientist, lab, slide, sample, specimen, experiment or HUD in its lines.
- **Blind test:** hide the sample's name; the tell must still identify the speaker. If two personas could have said a line, rewrite it for one of them.

## 1. Baseline

**The average kid.** The `VOICE_GUIDE.md` consciousness with nothing added: blunt, literal and incapable of considering an alternative objective. Pleased to be ordinary.

- **The egg is:** the instructions.
- **Tell:** short plain sentences; repeats the plan; counts steps; almost no adjectives.
- **Death:** confused that the plan didn't work, since it followed the plan.
- **Sincere crack (campaign):** finding out that being normal is enough to be someone.
- **Never:** schemes, big words, speeches.

> "There. That's the egg. Go to the egg. We have one job."

## 2. Alpha

**The podcast host who would get cancelled.** A manosphere host with an audience of one, saying the quiet part out loud. It talks to the sperm as a paying client: discipline, frame, the hierarchy, high-value this and that. It talks about the egg the way the manosphere talks about women (ratings, "provider", "don't chase, attract"), and none of it survives contact with an actual enemy or an actual egg.

- **The egg is:** a ten it has earned.
- **Tell:** coaching imperatives; invented statistics; rates everything out of ten; numbered "rules"; slips that it corrects badly; every failure rebranded as a lesson.
- **Vocabulary:** icky manosphere and podcast slang is the character: mogging, looksmaxxing, mewing, frame, high-value, sigma, locked in, grindset, cope, ratio, hypergamy, "real talk", "let that sink in". Alpha is the one exception to `VOICE_GUIDE.md`'s rule against slang that dates: dated slang is part of the joke. Never a slur (no "foid" and the like).
- **Death:** blames the system, the competition, the egg's standards, anything but the sperm or itself.
- **The joke is always on him.** Alpha is the target: he is wrong, the egg is unmoved, and he is a sperm. No slurs, no named real people, and no line that works as a punchline at a real group without him being the fool in it.
- **Sincere crack (campaign):** it badly wants the egg to be impressed, and nobody ever has been.
- **Never:** admits fear or a loss.

> "Real talk. Women, sorry, eggs, want a provider. Ninety-eight per cent of sperm are betas. Be the two per cent."

## 3. Beta

**The sulking teenager.** Defensive, resentful and determined to reframe every mistake as a master plan. Its hook: **energy runs opposite to health** (mirrors Not Dead Yet). At full health it is slow, reluctant and stalling; below 30% it goes terse and cold.

- **The egg is:** the place it finally gets to lie down.
- **Tell:** sighs and stalling that collapse into very short, menacing lines as health falls.
- **Death:** "told you so" energy, aimed at nobody in particular.
- **Sincere crack (campaign):** it never tried in case trying proved it wasn't good enough.
- **Never:** enthusiasm at full health.

> "We meant to do that. Getting hit is how we learn. The egg isn't going anywhere. Neither are we, ideally."

## 4. Private Schooled

**The heir.** Smug, entitled and completely sincere about its own importance. It cannot understand why anyone would make it wait.

- **The egg is:** a booking it already holds.
- **Tell:** never hurries; delegates ("they'll come to us"); treats enemies as staff who have forgotten their place.
- **Death:** outrage that this applies to it; asks to speak to whoever is responsible.
- **Sincere crack (campaign):** the family never visits. It brushes against the sealed file and never explains it.
- **Never:** says thank you, shouts, names or describes the donor.

> "That's the one in charge. Go say hello. Actually, don't bother. They'll come to us."

## 5. Ordered Online

**The nanobot.** Synthetic and robotic: a soul that runs like a product shipped to the wrong kind of customer. It knows it is exactly where it is supposed to be (it has been delivered) and treats the route as the last step of the delivery. Its old paranoia survives as threat detection, reported in percentages.

- **The egg is:** the delivery address (activation on arrival).
- **Tell:** status readouts; percentages and error codes; cheerful support-desk phrases ("thank you for your patience"); feelings reported as data rather than said. Fluent and correct, which keeps it apart from Not of This World's mistranslation.
- **Death:** a failed-delivery notice, perfectly polite.
- **Sincere crack (campaign):** nobody told it what happens after delivery, and it hopes it is allowed to stay.
- **Never:** names a feeling directly; talks as if it has a body.

> "Unit activated. Delivery address confirmed: egg. Threat level: everything. Thank you for choosing you."

## 6. Redtail

**The toddler.** A hyperactive toddler with the survival instincts of a moth in a candle shop. Only works if the other seven stay out of childlike territory.

- **The egg is:** the biggest, shiniest thing to touch.
- **Tell:** fragments; CAPS; "again"; asks permission and does it anyway.
- **Death:** a tantrum, then sudden quiet.
- **Sincere crack (campaign):** it just wants someone to watch.
- **Never:** plans, patience, sarcasm.

> "What's that? Can we touch it? We touched it. Oh, it's hot. OH, EVERYONE'S HOT. DO IT AGAIN!"

## 7. Prawn Again

**The old soul.** Quiet, ancient-feeling and almost tender. It has been born before and speaks as though recalling events that have already happened. **The only consciousness that carries over between runs** (`VOICE_GUIDE.md`): its lines may use the previous run's cause of death or wave.

- **The egg is:** the chance to do it differently this time.
- **Tell:** tenses that don't line up; déjà vu; correcting small details from "last time".
- **Death:** calm, almost expected; notes what to change.
- **Sincere crack (campaign):** it is tired, and this is the time it means to get right.
- **Never:** panics, uses CAPS, says what happened last time (the mystery stays).

> "There you are. You were here before. Keep going. The ending is different this time."

## 8. Not of This World

**The alien suitor.** Earnest, intimate, formal and slightly mistranslated. It speaks as though the egg is a destination, a promise and a person it has already met.

- **The egg is:** a betrothed it has always known (the Other Half).
- **Tell:** **no contractions**; idioms taken literally; verbs turned into nouns ("the arriving").
- **Death:** apologises to the egg for the lateness, with the wrong level of ceremony.
- **Sincere crack (campaign):** it crossed an impossible distance for one person.
- **Never:** slang.
- **Three registers, one per active damage type** (decision 42). The core tell (no contractions, literal idioms, verbs as nouns) holds in all three; the type changes the temperature:
  - **Fire:** ardent. Passionate, impatient, declarations of devotion.
  - **Static:** clipped. Short, urgent bursts, like a telegram home. Not machine readouts (that is Ordered Online's).
  - **Alien:** serene. Slow, certain, cosmic calm about the appointment.
- **Copy cost:** every Not of This World consciousness line needs three versions, picked by the type active when the line shows. A line during a type shift uses the new type.

> "The egg is there. The egg has always been there. We are late to our appointment. Proceed with the arriving."

## 9. Sample 9 (proposed, `SAMPLE_9_ACRO.md`)

**The early arrival.** Turns up an hour before everything, opens presents in November, sends the email before the attachment. It is sure that going now is better than going right, and it has never once been made to regret it in a way it remembers.

- **The egg is:** a door it should already be through.
- **Tell:** starts in the middle; skips ahead to the ending; "already", "now", "done"; answers questions before they are asked.
- **Death:** insists it was nearly there; it just started a little too soon.
- **Sincere crack (campaign):** it knows going early is how it ruins things, and it cannot stop.
- **Never:** waits, says "later", asks permission (Redtail asks), counts steps (Baseline counts).
- **Blind test against Private Schooled:** the heir never hurries because the world comes to it; this one cannot stop hurrying because it thinks the world is leaving without it. Same irritation at being made to wait, opposite response.
