# Spawn Prawn

Autorun, autogun bullet-storm roguelite for Android. You are Spermy, an alien spermatozoon in a race against four hundred million rival swimmers and the host's entire immune system. Get the sperm count down to one, win the Final Five showdown, and fertilise the egg.

**Install:** sideload `release/SpawnPrawn.apk` (Android 5.0+). Enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager when prompted.

## Features

- **Seventeen signature weapons.** Fewer weapons, each a different way to play: Spitball (marksman), Hiccup Scattergun (brawler), Yo-Yo Diet (boomerang), Slipstream Scalpel (your swim path cuts), Heartburn (flamethrower), Nappy Mines (trapper), Cold Feet (freezer), Static Cling (chain lightning), Morning Sickness (toxic puddles), Tapeworm Seeder (necromancer), Seeker Siblings (homing swarm), Toddler Gravity (black holes), Premature Evangelation (guardian angels who get started way too soon), Placental Siphon (eats enemy bullets and spits them back), and three melee weapons: Placenta Paddle (big sweeping swings that knock crowds flying), Flagellum Flail (your tail cracks like a whip in a long line) and Thorny Onesie (spikes pulse out all around you, harder the more max HP and armour you have).
- **Upgrade paths of their own.** Lv 3 offers upgrades any weapon can take. Lv 5 and Lv 8 (signatures) and Lv 10 (mastery) each fork into two upgrades only that weapon has, 102 in all. Only one weapon a run can reach mastery: once one hits Lv 10, the others stop at Lv 9. Examples: Hock a Loogie or Wet Willy, then Kidney Stone (a railgun) or Projectile Vomit (a hose); Closing the Loop (swim a circle round enemies and everything inside gets cut); Walking Dead (infected corpses rise as zombie allies); Power Grid (every weapon you own can set off Static Cling); Nuclear Nappy; Black Hole Yo-Yo; Mirror Womb; and so on. The Lv 8 forks are build-defining too: Recoil Jump (the Scattergun kicks you clear of every blast), Cat's Cradle (a cutting string to every yo-yo), Slipstream, Napalm, Claymore, Homing Nappies, Ball Lightning, Geyser, Hive Mind, Swarm Intelligence, Crush Depth, Smite, Martyrdom, Ground Pound, Snap Back, Porcupine, Fortress and more. The Armoury marks them ONLY HERE and MASTERY.
- **Readable late game.** Your own shots, trails, puddles, glows and sparks fade back automatically as the screen fills up (down to a quarter strength), draw underneath enemies, and damage numbers thin out (crits always show). Enemies and enemy bullets are never faded. Settings > Your effects: AUTO, FADED or FULL.
- **Acrosome Ram.** A power-up that turns your head into a weapon: enemies you swim into take big damage that grows with your level, max HP and armour, four times as much at full speed, where it also sends out a shockwave and softens their contact damage by 40% (HUNT autorun loves it).
- **Tank builds.** Big Boned (more max HP, and damage from every 100 max HP you have), Prickly Personality (thorns: whatever hurts you gets hurt back) and Stubborn Streak (below half health, take less and deal more) stack with Thick Skin, Shell Suit, Acrosome Ram and the Thorny Onesie.
- **Seven rarities.** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythical and Celestial, each with its own colour. Legendary and up glow, wear a ribbon, get a light sweep and announce themselves when the box opens. Mythical (pulsing red) and Celestial (prismatic) also carry a unique bonus effect for the rest of the run, and only turn up once or twice a run (three at most): Blood Moon, Storm Crown, Phoenix Down, Growth Hormone, Pocket Black Hole, Bullet Time, Supernova, Hand of God, Twin Soul, State of Grace, Starfall.
- **Sample 002: The Petri Dish (waves).** A mad scientist is breeding super sperm. One drop goes into the dish at a time, wave after wave: no DNA opens mid-wave; clear it, open everything you earned, then press START WAVE. From wave 2 every drop has a side effect (a run event), DIRE from wave 10, and every 5th wave drops in a boss. Stragglers are pipetted back to you so every wave can be finished. Your score is how many waves you clear.
- **Upgrade previews.** On an upgrade path, the animated preview shows the highlighted upgrade itself in action: the nuke every 3rd Nappy, the second ring of angels, the hose of Projectile Vomit, the Spin Cycle, Cat's Cradle's string, Porcupine's spines and so on, for all 132 upgrades.
- **Run events.** From level 6 something unexpected happens every couple of minutes: Feeding Frenzy, Glass Womb (double damage both ways), Sugar Rush, Bullet Hell, Kidney Stone Shower, The Horde, a fleeing Golden Swimmer carrying a chest, a Most Wanted bounty, Lights Out, Waters Breaking (a current sweeps everything one way) and Mitosis (everything that dies splits in two). From level 40 they turn DIRE: more often, nastier, better paid, and sometimes two at once.
- **Upgrades with a twist.** When a power-up or branch would do nothing for a weapon, that weapon gets its own version instead, and the card says so. Spoilers makes the Slipstream Scalpel blink you straight through an enemy, drops Nappy Mines already under enemies, lands Morning Sickness globs before you throw them, starts Static Cling bolts from the far side of the crowd, has angels pop up next to enemies and puts returned Siphon shots right beside their target. Split Personality makes the Scalpel's blade bigger and longer; Pushy adds chain jumps, bigger puddles, shoving blasts and trails; Last Word, Tactical Nap, Tunnel Vision, Twitchy Tail, Bigger Load, Short Refractory Period and Early Arrival all have weapon-specific versions too.
- **Pairings.** Eighteen secret combos between two weapons you own at Lv 5+: lightning sets off your mines (Baby Monitor Network), angels catch bullets for the Siphon (Collection Plate), gravity orbs freeze what they hold (Snow Globe), turrets fire Seeker Siblings (Family Tree), your scalpel trail drops mines (Trail Mix). Each is ??? until you find it; the Codex keeps the ones you have.
- **Weapon Drafts.** Three weapons a run: one at level 1, then drafts at levels 8 and 22, on their own full-screen draft: a live animated preview of each weapon in action, tabs (or swipe) between the three choices, its role, power/fire rate/reach/crowd bars, the six signature and mastery playstyles it unlocks, its pairings and your current mounts. Weapon upgrade paths (Lv 3, 5, 8, 10) open the same showcase, focused on the weapon you're upgrading. In ordinary DNA strands, weapon level-ups sit in their own UPGRADE A WEAPON section with a live mini preview, the level track and what the upgrade unlocks. Recycling a weapon drafts a replacement.
- **Eight bosses, four per run.** You never know which four you'll get, or in what order. Each arrives with a cinematic: the world stops, the camera swims over and pushes in, it roars, and its file card slams in with its quote, strengths and weaknesses, and the three relics it guards (BOSS 2 OF 4 THIS RUN). The Macrophage Queen eats her minions to heal; the Antibody Colossus is armour you can't move; the Immune Eye teleports and glares a tracking beam (and takes double damage while it does); the Matron heals everything behind a ring of nurses; the Pepsinator rains acid and splits; Chad Prime dashes three times and then has to catch his breath; the Fever is immune to fire and rages at 35%; Mitch & Osis rebuild each other unless you finish both within 8 seconds.
- **Boss relics.** A boss dies in slow motion, then you choose one of its three relics, 24 in all, each meant to define a build: Swallow Whole (touching small enemies eats them), The Queen's Court (three macrophage bodyguards), Border Wall, Bouncer, Diplomatic Immunity, Death Stare (your own tracking beam), Triage, Acid Blood, Tail Whip, Sprint Start, Running Hot, Heatstroke, Mirror Twin, Twin Pick (take two cards from every DNA strand) and more. The Codex's boss ward records who you've met.

- **Greyscale until stained.** The slide itself (the creatures, the field and the on-screen readouts) is greyscale; the menus, cards and weapon slots keep their colour. Colour comes back one kind at a time through Stain cards in your DNA strands, the way a biologist adds a dye to see one protein: **GFP Tag** (you, your shots and allies glow green; always offered in your first level-ups), **Anti-Immune Stain** (anything that hurts you turns red), **Luciferase** (DNA, elites, bosses and big amoebas glow gold), **Motility Dye** (fast swimmers get a cyan label), **Rival Dyes** (each rival wears their colour) and the **H&E Stain Kit** (power-up pickups, their effects, and your weapon-type colour on your midpiece). Each stain also brings a boon that fits it: GFP +12% damage (you can see where your shots land), Anti-Immune +8% dodge, Luciferase +20% luck and +25% damage to elites and bosses, Motility +8% swim speed and +30% damage to fast enemies, Rival Dyes +40% damage to rival champions, H&E +30% pickup range and a reroll. The pause BUILD tab lists what you've stained and its boon.
- **Fewer, heavier bullets.** Shooters are rarer and every volley fires 3 of each 5 shots (patterns keep their shape), each 1.7x as damaging. Enemy shots look like real debris under the microscope: small dark granules with a thin bright halo (with the Anti-Immune Stain their cores take up the red dye).
- **Loot cards show everything.** No more hold for details: every card has its full description.
- **Choose sperm sample.** START SWIMMING opens a sample rack styled as microscope slides (frosted label, a drop under the coverslip). Sample #001 Standard Issue is in stock; Frozen Donor Bank, The Morning After and Vasectomy Reversal are coming soon. More to cum.
- **The crowd.** A steady stream of tiny, harmless sperm swims in from beyond the arena edge and heads for the egg, where they cluster and try to tunnel in. Nothing targets them, but any shot or blast that touches one pops it. They're there to make the 400 million feel real.
- **You drive the sperm count.** It sits at 400,000,000 until your first kill (a monster or one of the crowd). After that every kill takes a chunk off: the count flashes green and the drop floats up from where you're fighting ("-2.4M"). It falls mostly through your kills and your growth, a little with time.
- **New goal: sperm count to one.** No more level-60 gate or membrane to break: the sperm count falls to six, you beat the Final Five, then fertilise the egg (see below).
- **Loot is DNA.** Level-ups, elites, bosses, rivals and achievements give you strands of DNA instead of boxes: gold double helices spinning on the floor, and on the loot screen a strand that wriggles and unzips rung by rung (with a zipper of tiny snaps) before you splice in one of three genes. Fan DNA and Boss DNA are Epic or better.
- **Yeast infection.** From about 3:30 (then every 5 to 7 minutes) a Candida infection breaks out just off screen: three oval yeast cells that each bud a daughter every 6 to 8 seconds, chained together by pseudohyphae, so an ignored colony doubles and doubles (up to 70 cells). Colonies are sticky (30% slower while you brush through). Kill every last cell to be cured, and get a Gold strand of DNA for it. Chips show the cell count and when you're stuck.
- **The morning-after pill.** From about 4 to 7 minutes in (and every 5 to 7 minutes after), a pill drops somewhere random with a warning, fizzes, then dissolves into an organically shaped cloud of wobbling lobes that grows for over a minute until it covers about half the map, holds, and slowly dissipates. Inside it you and the rivals swim 35% slower and you gain half the XP. It shows on the minimap, the autopilot steers around it, and a PILL chip lights up while you're in it.
- **Tail snipping.** Shots that cross a swimmer's flagellum, or hit it from behind, cut the tail off: the swimmer can only twitch and drift, and the severed tail wriggles away and fades. (Not rivals or bosses.)
- **Your tail grows.** Spermy's flagellum gets longer every level, about 2.5x by level 60, with more links so it stays smooth.
- **Denser swarms.** About 1.7x as many monsters on screen, each a bit weaker (65% health, 80% damage, 60% XP each), so it's a proper swarm without being harder.
- **Six more pond creatures:**
  - **Pinworm** (from 2:20): a long ringed worm whose body follows its head through every turn.
  - **Diatom** (3:00): a glass pillbox with radial ribs; armoured, slow, fires rings of six.
  - **Water Bear** (4:30): a plump tardigrade on eight stubby legs. Very tough, can't be knocked back, and once per life curls into a 'tun' for 2.5 s, taking almost no damage.
  - **Paramecium** (1:15): a slipper-shaped ciliate that swims in long straight lines, then backs off and turns after bumping you.
  - **Rotifer** (1:50): spinning wheel organs hoover up your XP off the floor. Kill it to get it all back with 30% interest.
  - **Volvox** (2:40): a rolling hollow ball colony that bursts into four daughter colonies.
- **New wave events:** KRILL SHOAL (three shoals at once) and POND LIFE (a Volvox, a school of Paramecia and two Rotifers).
- **Faster menus.** Action buttons stay pinned to the bottom of long screens. Tap acts, press-and-hold shows details: loot cards show a one-line summary (hold for the full text) and holding a weapon slot shows its stats without opening the Armoury. The pause screen opens on RUN (autorun and the egg) with BUILD, THE SHOW and CODEX tabs. The Armoury's upgrade tree shows only where you are and your next milestone (SHOW FULL TREE for the rest), and you can swipe left and right between slots.
- **Where your loot came from.** Every box tells its own little story: the elite you went through the pockets of, the Amoeba it was fished out of, the rival whose mum wants it back, the sponsor, the boss's will, the achievement, the level.
- **Edge warnings.** Red chevrons on the screen edge point at off-screen shooters and flash just before they fire.
- **COPY THIS RUN** on the game-over screen copies that run's summary for balancing.
- **You are Spermy.** The race board, game-over screens and the System all call you by name.
- **Krill.** Small translucent crustaceans in shoals of a dozen, from 0:45: curled segmented bodies, big dark eyes, twitching antennae and swimmerets that paddle as they flick-swim in bursts, changing heading each kick. Fragile, fast and annoying. Nobody knows how they got in.
- **Run log.** Every run (win, loss, or quit after 30 s) is summarised on the phone: result, time, level, kills, what ended it, what hurt most, damage share by weapon, the final build, rival outcomes, DNA strands opened and your level and HP% at every minute. Settings > Run log > COPY RUN LOG copies the last 60 runs as text for balancing. Nothing leaves the phone unless you copy it.
- **DNA strand sound.** The box rattles as it shakes, the latch clicks and the lid pops, then a glassy chime plays that gets longer and brighter with the best rarity inside (Bronze 2 notes up to Legendary 5 plus a shimmer), with a soft swish as each card flies in. Branch choices ring softer, Boss DNA thud deeper, and a cursed card adds a sour low note.
- **Pinch to zoom.** Two fingers (or the mouse wheel) zoom from 24x to 80x. A microscope objective gauge shows the magnification while you pinch, and the scale bar and readout follow it. Spawn distances ignore zoom, so zooming in never brings monsters closer. The image blurs and hunts back into focus after each change, like turning a real fine-focus knob, with ratchet clicks as it turns (brighter zooming in, lower zooming out) and a heavier click when it settles. Your zoom is remembered.
- **Opening shot.** The title screen sits in front of an out-of-focus lab (window blinds, shelf of flasks, petri dishes, a SPAWN-O-SCOPE 40x microscope). Press START and focus pulls onto the microscope, the camera dives into the eyepiece and the slide opens out into the game, with sound: focus-knob ticks, a glass slide sliding across the stage and clacking into its clips, the objective turret clunking into place and an airy swell as the view opens. Tap to skip.
- **Everything is a joke.** Every weapon, spell, power-up and branch perk is reproductive-biology comedy: Kidney Stone Railgun, Cold Feet, Helicopter Parent, Nappy Mines, Gender Reveal Blaster, Stork Drop, Latex Barrier (98% effective), Dutch Oven, Protein Shake, Short Refractory Period and so on.
- **Settings screen** (title and pause menus), all saved on the device: **Darkfield microscope** (black field, bright specimens), **High specimen detail** (mitochondrial helix, vacuoles, granules, nucleus outlines, a proper zona breach), **Clean clinical view** (no grain, lens blur, vignette or halos), depth of field, **Minimal HUD** (one thin top bar, messages as toasts), **CASA Pro panel**, **Layout** (auto, portrait, landscape), narrator and sound.
- **Your upgrades show on your body.** Split Personality grows extra flagella (up to 4), Thick Skin and Shell Suit make the head bigger and plated, Leg Day (Tail Day) and Hydrodynamic Head stretch the head and lengthen the tail, Twitchy Tail quickens the beat, Sticky Cilia sprouts rippling cilia, Crit grows a barb, Protein Shake swells the acrosome, element passives dye the midpiece in their type colour (fire, shock and arcane also glow), Clingy adds a receptor field, Pregnancy Vitamins and Leech Mode a pulsing droplet, Lucky Swimmer a gold speck.
- **Darkfield** is a true AMOLED black field with only the faintest out-of-focus specks behind the specimens.
- **CASA Pro panel.** Live computer-assisted sperm analysis on yourself: VCL, VSL, LIN, ALH and BCF from the last second of your head track, a WHO motility grade (A rapid progressive to D immotile), a histogram of what's on the slide and a timestamped track log (level-ups, elites, bosses, big amoebas, rivals).
- **Landscape and tablet layout.** Weapons and spells in a column down the left, boss and egg bars top centre, a bigger minimap that scales with the screen, Rewind bottom right. Auto switches when the screen is wide; the Android app rotates to match (or locks to your chosen orientation).
- **Narrator packs.** The System, a nature documentary narrator, a disappointed midwife on a double shift, or the rivals' mums.
- **Modifier Forge.** Five new modifiers (Chaining, Pulsing, Magnetic, Delayed, Mirror) and 8 Duo Combos that trigger when two modifiers share a weapon (Cluster Hunter, Cryoblast, Halo, Snowball, Pinball Wizard, Pied Piper, Kaleidoscope, Time Bomb).
- **Gene Bank (meta progression).** Every run banks DNA (levels, bosses, rival kills, time survived, a big bonus for being born). Spend it on inherited traits (max HP, damage, XP, rerolls, traction, pickup range, an extra Rewind charge), extra starter weapons (one is always offered in your first box) and green-family tag dyes.
- **Rival champions race you to the egg.** Five named rivals (Big Steve, Chad Flagellum, Professor Wiggles, Lil' Zygo, Kevin) grow elsewhere on a much larger map, each with their own growth speed and temper. They zap monsters (stealing your XP), pick fights when they're close to your level, flee when hurt and regenerate, and give you a wide berth once you outgrow them. Their toughness tracks your recent damage output, so a rival your size is always a proper duel. Whoever of them is still alive at the end joins the Final Five. Killing one drops a Fan DNA and about a level of XP; the host occasionally eliminates one off-screen. A race board under the minimap shows the standings, with rival dots on the minimap and pointers to anyone hunting you or breaking in.
- **Sample 000: Lab Bench (debug).** A test level: nothing spawns on its own, no rivals, no events, no bosses unless you send them. The DEBUG button (under the minimap) opens a panel: god mode, freeze time, loot boxes on or off, +5 levels, heal, clear enemies; send any enemy type (x1, x5 or x20, optionally elite); spawn any boss; start any run event (normal or DIRE); switch any weapon or spell on or off and level it up or down.
- **Enemies look different from each other.** Swimmers that share a body get their own build (Sprinters are long and fast-beating, Spermlets tiny, Quantum Swimmers flicker inside a field, Headbutters have big barbed heads, Ghost Swimmers trail two tails, the Alpha Swimmer is armoured and hairy). Cells too: Macrophages reach out with pseudopods, Mitotic Cells pinch in two around spindle fibres, Mucus Walls ooze in layers, Mother Cells swell buds before a brood, Killer T-Cells carry blades, Plasmodia pulse with veins, Acid Bubbles throb faster when you're close, Nurse Cells wear a halo, Cytokine Casters have orbiting motes and Enzyme Spires turning crystal. Individuals vary a little in size, soft bodies breathe, and elites have orbiting gold markers.
- **Epic boss fights.** Bosses arrive in a pillar of light with a shockwave that shoves everything back. While they live the slide's edges darken and pulse, a ground sigil of counter-rotating rings turns beneath them, energy spirals into them and they flare whenever they fire. At 66% and 33% health they enrage (banner, shockwave, glowing cracks, a faster sigil, a red health bar). The health bar trails the damage you deal, marks the phases and jolts on big hits. Deaths are a slow-motion chain of bursts ending in a white blast and a column of light.
- **Effects.** Hits throw sparks in their element's style (white streaks, embers, ice shards, goo, arcane motes), crits flare a star, explosions open with a white-hot flash then sparks, smoke and embers, lightning forks and crackles with a white-hot core, guns have muzzle flashes and shots leave motion streaks, flames leave smoke, deaths splatter goo (elites and bosses with a shockwave). Spells got their own: Stork Drop falls out of the sky with a shadow, Cold Shower punches out a ring of ice spikes, Brainstorm lights up the screen, Kiss It Better sends up green crosses, Latex Barrier is a shimmering hex membrane, Nap Time ripples out from you, black holes wind spiral arms and gas clouds roll. Frozen enemies grow an ice crust, shocked ones crackle, poisoned ones bubble. All of it thins out when the screen is busy.
- **Element colours.** Lightning is always drawn in a static-shock blue and pink, and toxic puddles and clouds keep a faint green even before you find a stain. The H&E Stain Kit brings back the rest: fire orange, frost blue, toxic green and arcane violet. The Motility Dye stains fast swimmers themselves cyan.
- **Deliberate colour.** The world and the whole UI are greyscale. Colour means exactly one thing each: GFP green is you (your sperm, your shots, your echoes, your mind-controlled allies), red is anything that can hurt you (enemy bullets, acid, hits you take, low HP), gold is reward (loot, big XP, elites, charged mitochondria), and each rival wears its own dye. The UI adds two more, taken from patient-monitor conventions (every trace has its own fixed colour): monitor cyan for upgrades (anything that permanently changes your build: weapons, levels, branch perks, modifiers, power-up stats) and monitor magenta for pickups (temporary field power-ups and their active effects). DNA strands are gold in the world and in the UI; card rarity shows as border weight rather than colour. The renderer enforces it: any other colour is drawn as grey.
- **Weapon icons and type colours.** Every weapon and spell has its own line icon (syringe Booster Jab, neuron Nervous Breakdown, biohazard Nappy Bomb, thermometer Thermometer Lance, medical cross Kiss It Better, and so on), coloured by damage type in the UI: Kinetic steel, Fire orange, Frost periwinkle, Shock lemon, Toxic chartreuse, Arcane violet (chosen to stay clear of the meaning colours). Shown on the weapon slots and their ammo bars, the Armoury, and loot cards. In the world, your shots stay GFP green.
- **Medical-device UI.** Styled on radiology workstations and patient monitors: black panels, 1px hairlines, square corners, monospace uppercase readouts, no glows or gradients. HP is a vital-sign module with a heart rate that climbs as you're hurt and a live ECG trace (your green; red when low; flat when you die). Radar-style minimap, elapsed-time readout, a lead "R" side marker as on a radiograph, film-sheet loot cards. Finished like real film: rounded sheets lifted by soft drop shadows, blue-black with uneven exposure and grain, edges that catch the light and fade rather than hard outlines, whites with a little blue in them. Panels are pre-rendered and nothing animated or blended sits over the live game view, so it stays fast on phones; a frame-rate readout sits next to the scale bar.
- **Glow only where light is emitted.** Fire and heat, electricity, arcane and time effects, fluorescent dye tags, laser beams and charged mitochondria glow. Bullets, needles, blades, drones, ice, poison, acid, XP, pickups, enemy bullets and every creature's body do not; physical shots are drawn solid with a dark edge. Colourless creatures are darker, with stronger halos, for contrast.
- **Looks like a real microscope.** The whole game is drawn as a live wet mount under positive phase contrast, the standard view for semen analysis: an even grey-green field brightest in the middle of the camera frame, counting-chamber grid lines, out-of-focus particles as soft discs with dark diffraction rings, and every object darker than the fluid with a bright halo round its edge. Sperm have real proportions (flat oval head with a paler acrosome, short midpiece, a hair-thin tail about ten head-lengths long). You are GFP-tagged (your acrosome glows green); each rival wears its own fluorescent dye. White blood cells show lobed nuclei and granules; amoebas have a clear rim, granular insides, a nucleus, a pulsing contractile vacuole and food vacuoles; the egg is a proper oocyte (granular ooplasm, germinal vesicle, glassy zona pellucida, polar body, corona radiata and cumulus). A CASA-style overlay (as in computer-assisted sperm analysis) draws colour-coded motion tracks and detection brackets, with a 20 um scale bar and objective readout. The UI is greyscale imaging-software styling.
- **DNA strands.** A strand on every level up (Common or better), plus about 45 more on a full run: Fan DNA from kills (Epic or better, at most one every 9 seconds; elites and big amoebas are the usual source), Boss DNA, rival kills and achievement DNA. Once the egg is yours to break, half of your weapons' target picks in range go to it.
- **Amoebas.** Big, slow, spongy engulfers (Amoeba from 1:10, Plasmodium from 4:00) that are immune to knockback, knit themselves back together, and absorb any smaller monster they touch, taking its health, bulk and XP. Leave one alone and it gets enormous; kill a big one for a Fan DNA and all the XP it swallowed. A Plasmodium splits into amoebas when it dies.
- **Swim physics.** Your head can only turn so fast, thrust drops mid-turn, and sideways momentum drifts off instead of stopping dead, so sharp turns carve arcs (you pivot faster when nearly stopped). **Traction** is a stat: Sticky Cilia and Hydrodynamic Head upgrades sharpen turns and cut drift. Tails are physical chains that drag and sweep behind every swimmer. You grow 1.5% bigger every level (your hitbox grows half as fast).
- **Terrain.** The womb is full of things: Cartilage Nodules (solid; every shot, yours and theirs, bounces off), Mitochondria (solid; soak up shots and vent an ATP Burst when full, which hits monsters, wipes bullets and gives you an ATP rush if you're close), Acid Crypts (burn anything that touches them and melt shots), Cilia Beds (shove bodies and bend shots outwards), Tubal Currents (carry everything along) and Lubricant Slicks (your grip drops to 30%, so you drift). The autopilot steers around the dangerous ones.
- **Depth of field.** Background layers blur by depth, debris drifts out of focus above the focal plane, and the screen edges get a gentle lens blur (toggle in Settings).
- **Sperm count and the Final Five.** A sperm count starts at 400,000,000 and only ever falls once you make your first kill: with every kill (monsters, rival swimmers and popped crowd sperm), with your growth (full at level 60) and a little with time, on a log scale, so the millions melt away early and the last hundred feel tense. At six (you and five), the Final Five arrive all at once from every side: every rival champion still alive, fully grown and healed, plus stand-ins (The Dark Horse, Anonymous Donor, The Favourite, Mr Motility, The Underdog) for any you already eliminated. They never run and never heal; the monster spawns go quiet while you fight. Each finalist you kill takes one off the count. At one, the egg's zona opens: swim into it to fertilise it and win. Your fastest fertilisation is saved.
- **Autorun + autogun.** Weapons and spells fire on their own, each with its own cooldown, magazine and reload. You draft your weapons: one at level 1 and a new weapon mount at levels 8 and 22 (3 in total). Your swimmer visibly grows as you level up.
- **Programmable targeting directives** per weapon/spell: Nearest, Strongest, Weakest, Lowest Health, Highest Health, Highest Armour, Fastest, Furthest, Densest Cluster, Elites & Bosses, Shooters First, Random, Revenge.
- **Armoury:** tap any weapon slot to open it. Tabs for every slot (locked ones show the level they unlock at), stat tiles, level pips, the upgrade tree, the 3 modifier slots with their power, a directive grid with a one-line explanation of each, the weapon's pairings, and Recycle (frees the slot for 2 rerolls).
- **Autorun directives:** Kite, Collect, Orbit, Hunt, Hold, Nest. The pilot scores 16 escape directions against predicted enemy and bullet positions. Drag anywhere to steer manually.
- **DNA strand on every level up:** pick 1 of 3 (new weapon, new spell, upgrade, power-up or fusion), with Bronze/Silver/Gold/Legendary rarities and rerolls. Elites drop DNA strands; bosses drop Gold+ boxes.
- **Modifiers** install into one weapon (3 slots each), with power set by the card's rarity. Picking one you already have powers it up (to a cap). Seeking, Splitting (shots shatter into shards on first hit), Orbiting (shots circle you eating enemy bullets, then launch), Growing (shots swell in size and damage as they fly), Boomerang, Ricochet, Freezing, Exploding, Mind Control (monsters fight for you, up to 6 at once), Element Swap, Shrapnel.
- **New power-ups:** Last Word, Tactical Nap, Tunnel Vision, Overachiever, Pincer Movement, Hurry Up, Anchor Link, Spoilers, Inheritance. **Cursed cards** give big boons with real downsides.
- **The Show:** a snarky System announcer, 38 achievements (some with real rewards: DNA strands, rerolls, scrap, healing), a live viewer counter, and sponsors who drop gifts at viewer milestones. DNA strands come in Bronze, Silver, Gold and Legendary.
- **Elemental reactions:** Thermal Shock, Steam Burst, Combustion, Toxic Arc, Superconduct, Resonance, Overload. Owning 2+ of an element unlocks a synergy bonus.
- **Fewer, stronger enemies:** about half as many monsters on screen, each bigger and tougher, getting steadily nastier until 15:00.
- **27 monster types + 8 bosses** with bullet-hell patterns. Krill shoals, rival swimmers (Sprinters, Headbutters, Quantum and Ghost Swimmers, the armoured Alpha Swimmer, Spermlet swarms) and the immune system (Macrophages, Antibodies, Killer T-Cells, Mitotic Cells that split, Acid Bubbles, Nurse Cells, Mucus Walls, Cytokine Casters, Mother Cells, Enzyme Spires).
- **Field power-ups:** Magnet, Acid Flush, Adrenaline, Glucose Hit, Shield, Stasis, Loot Box.
- **The egg's glow** heals you while you stay near it; the NEST autorun directive keeps you there.
- **Time travel: Rewind and Paradox Echoes.** Tap REWIND to jump 4 seconds into the past (VHS-style rewind of the whole battle). Your future self does not vanish: it stays behind as a **Paradox Echo** that walks the erased timeline backwards, firing copies of your weapons, then collapses in a bullet-clearing blast. If you would die with a charge ready, Rewind triggers automatically. Charges refill from kills and bosses. Snooze Button adds charges.
- **Visuals:** living-tissue parallax (fluid, drifting cells, fibres), a warm womb floor that pulses with a heartbeat, a pearly egg that cracks as you break in, sperm with whipping tails, dynamic light from explosions, soft scorch decals, drop shadows and shading on every body, additive glow on projectiles and enemy bullets, vignette, damage flashes and a minimap.
- **About 10 minutes a run.** A boss every 2 minutes; the difficulty clock runs 1.5 times faster than real time, and if you level faster than a 10-minute run expects (level 60 at 9 minutes), enemies get 5% more health and 3% more damage for every level you're ahead. From 10:00 the **Immune Surge** makes enemies tougher and deadlier every minute.

## Build

Play in a browser: open `web/index.html` (WASD/arrows to steer, Esc to pause).

Build the APK (JDK 17+, Android SDK with platform 34):

```bash
cd storm-directive/android
echo "sdk.dir=/path/to/android-sdk" > local.properties
./gradlew assembleRelease
# -> app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk
```

The release build is signed with the local debug key so it installs directly. APKs built on different machines have different keys, so uninstall before installing one from another machine. GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/storm-directive-apk.yml`) builds the APK on every push that touches `storm-directive/`.
