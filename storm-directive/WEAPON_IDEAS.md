# Weapon ideas

Source: v9.21 guide. Damage types: Force, Acid, Mucus, Static, Cooties, Oil, Water, Blood, Soul.
Delight (1 to 10) means joke, visual surprise and "I'd show a friend", not power. Scores are a first-pass judgement.
Companion to `FEAT_IDEAS.md`.

## 1. Current weapons (33), as user stories

Ranked by usefulness within each group. Each line: name (damage type, role, sequence).

### Precision and single target
1. **Spitball** (Force, Marksman, Firstborn): As a player, I want reliable, accurate single shots, so that I always have steady damage on the nearest threat.
2. **Gene Gun** (Blood, Splicer, Designer Baby): As a player, I want fast precision shots that make enemies explode, so that single kills spill into the crowd.
3. **Seeker Siblings** (Blood, Swarm, Firstborn): As a player, I want homing shots that never miss and pick off the weakest, so that stragglers die without me aiming.
4. **Hand in Hand** (Force, Duellist, Twins): As a player, I want two homing shots that curve in from either side and meet on the target, so that my twin and I always hit together.
5. **Booger Flick** (Mucus, Saponifier, Favourite): As a player, I want piercing snot flicks that glue the fastest enemies in place, so that rushers stop rushing.
6. **Déjà Vu** (Soul, Repeater, Prawn Again): As a player, I want every shot to happen twice, so that each hit lands again as a memory.
7. **Ghosts of You** (Soul, Haunter, Prawn Again): As a player, I want homing ghosts of my past lives to haunt enemies, so that my history fights for me.
8. **Due Date** (Blood, Delayed Doom, Favourite): As a player, I want to stick a countdown on the toughest enemy that lands 40% of its damage again, so that big targets pay twice.

### Close range and melee
1. **Heartburn** (Acid, Acid Spray, Problem Child): As a player, I want a short-range reflux spray that melts everything it touches, so that anything close gets dissolved.
2. **Hiccup Scattergun** (Force, Brawler, Chonker): As a player, I want a close burst of pellets with knockback, so that I can blow a crowd off me.
3. **Shotgun Wedding** (Force, Brawler, Redtail): As a player, I want a double-barrelled blast with bouncing pellets, so that close fights end quickly.
4. **Placenta Paddle** (Force, Cleaver, Chonker): As a player, I want heavy sweeping swings that knock crowds flying, so that I can clear my space in one hit.
5. **Flagellum Flail** (Force, Lasher, Quiet One): As a player, I want my tail to crack like a whip in a straight line, so that I can hit a column of enemies at mid range.
6. **Thorny Onesie** (Force, Tank, Chonker): As a player, I want spikes to pulse out all around me, so that anything hugging me gets hurt.
7. **Yo-Yo Diet** (Force, Boomerang, Firstborn): As a player, I want a spinning blade that flies to the furthest enemy and comes back, so that it hits the whole line twice.

### Area and crowd control
1. **Static Cling** (Static, Chain Static, Bright Spark): As a player, I want instant static that arcs between enemies, so that clumps get zapped together.
2. **Toddler Gravity** (Soul, Crowd Control, Bright Spark): As a player, I want a slow orb that drags everything into it, so that I can bunch a crowd for my other weapons.
3. **Morning Sickness** (Cooties, Area Denial, Problem Child): As a player, I want to lob globs that leave puddles on the densest crowd, so that the ground itself fights for me.
4. **Moonshine Jug** (Oil, Firebomber, Redtail): As a player, I want to lob a jug that explodes and leaves a burning slick, so that crowds burn where they stand.
5. **Twin Telepathy** (Static, Geometry, Bright Spark / Twins): As a player, I want a beam joining me and my twin across the crowd, so that where we stand decides what gets zapped.
6. **Duelling Banjo** (Static, Ring, Redtail): As a player, I want a ring of 10 piercing notes around me, so that I hit everything nearby at once.
7. **Red Tape** (Cooties, Bureaucrat, Problem Child): As a player, I want to tape a target to its neighbours so a hit on one hurts the rest, so that one hit becomes four.
8. **Nappy Mines** (Acid, Trapper, Chonker): As a player, I want mines dropped in my wake, so that chasers walk into explosions.
9. **Skid Mark** (Oil, Boozy Trail, Quiet One): As a player, I want a trail that hurts whatever crosses it, so that swimming in circles becomes an attack.
10. **Colouring In** (Force, Lasso, Designer Baby): As a player, I want everything inside a loop I swim to get hit hard, so that my path becomes a weapon.

### Defence and payback
1. **Placental Siphon** (Water, Counter, Designer Baby): As a player, I want to eat enemy bullets near me and spit them back, so that bullet storms feed my damage.
2. **Premature Evangelation** (Soul, Bodyguard, Good Eater): As a player, I want guardian angels circling me that hit what comes close, so that I have a moving wall of damage.
3. **Karma** (Force, Payback, Prawn Again): As a player, I want every hit I take to come back as a ring of shots, so that getting hurt turns into damage.
4. **Bubble Wand** (Water, Trap & Throw, Good Eater): As a player, I want to trap small enemies in bubbles that burst when touched, so that I can park threats and pop them into the crowd.

### Summons and tricks
1. **Tapeworm Seeder** (Cooties, Necromancer, Good Eater): As a player, I want infected enemies to become turrets when they die, so that every kill adds to my army.
2. **Imaginary Friend** (Soul, Echo, Designer Baby): As a player, I want Gerald to copy my weapons a moment later, so that my damage repeats.
3. **Tooth Fairy** (Soul, Lure, Favourite): As a player, I want baby teeth dropped near crowds that smite whoever takes one, so that enemies walk into a trap for a reward.
4. **Peekaboo** (Soul, Trickster, Quiet One): As a player, I want to vanish so enemies swarm the empty spot, then BOO, so that I can turn a swarm into one big hit.

Current spread: Force 10, Soul 7, Blood 3, Cooties 3, Static 3, Acid 2, Oil 2, Water 2, Mucus 1.

Gaps worth filling: Mucus has one weapon; nothing tethers, charges up or builds a wall; only Booger Flick aims at the fastest enemy and only Yo-Yo Diet at the furthest; no free-roaming pet; no beam you hold on one target.

## 2. New weapon ideas (30), ranked by delight

Each line: name (damage type, role, suggested sequence, aims at), SCAMPER move, user story, reactions it sets up.

### Delight 9
1. **Sticky Fingers** (Mucus, Grabber, Favourite, FASTEST). Substitute Colouring In's loop with a party-bag sticky hand. As a player, I want a stretchy hand on a string that slaps the fastest enemy and reels it towards me, so that rushers get dragged into my other weapons. Reactions: Booger, Scab with Blood.
2. **Waters Breaking** (Water, Flood, Prawn Again, around you). Magnify Power Shower. As a player, I want a gush that floods outward from me every few seconds, washing enemies and bullets away, so that I get a sudden, panicked clear-out. Reactions: Waterlogged, Holy Water with Soul.
3. **Greased Piglet** (Oil, Ricochet pet, Redtail, free-roaming). Put Yo-Yo Diet to another use. As a player, I want a squealing greased piglet that skids wildly round the screen, bouncing off walls and enemies, so that I get a chaotic pet nobody can catch. Reactions: Slip 'n Slide, Grease Fire with Static.
4. **Soft Play** (Cooties, Zone, Prawn Again, DENSEST CLUSTER). Combine Morning Sickness and a ball pit. As a player, I want a ball pit dropped on the crowd that bogs enemies down and gives them Cooties, so that the germiest place on earth works for me. Reactions: Public Pool with Water, The Shakes with Static.

### Delight 8
1. **Egg White** (Mucus, Highway, Firstborn, ahead of you). Reverse Booger Flick (help yourself, not just stick them). As a player, I want a stretchy lane of fertile mucus laid ahead of me that speeds me and my shots up and traps enemies in it, so that I race along my own fast lane. Reactions: Snail Trail with Oil, Short Circuit with Static.
2. **Hanky Panky** (Mucus, Lasher, Quiet One, NEAREST). Adapt Flagellum Flail. As a player, I want to crack a snotty hanky like a towel in the changing room, gluing whatever it snaps, so that a whip hit also pins. Reactions: Booger, Heartburn with Acid.
3. **Juice Box** (Acid, Stream then lob, Problem Child, NEAREST). Combine Heartburn and Morning Sickness. As a player, I want to squeeze a juice box of stomach acid in a stream and then throw the empty box, which bursts, so that every reload ends with a bang. Reactions: Meltdown, Vinaigrette with Oil.
4. **Vomit Comet** (Acid, Spiral orbit, Redtail, around you). Adapt Premature Evangelation. As a player, I want a ball of sick orbiting me in a widening spiral that leaves acid splats, so that the space around me becomes a hazard. Reactions: Ulcer with Blood, Penicillin with Cooties.
5. **Rubber Duck** (Water, Squirting pets, Twins, NEAREST). Modify Tapeworm Seeder's turrets. As a player, I want a pair of rubber ducks that bob after me and squirt enemies (and quack when they hit), so that I get a little firing squad. Reactions: Toaster in the Bath with Static, Crime Scene with Blood.
6. **Umbilical Cord** (Blood, Tether, Twins, HIGHEST HEALTH). Adapt Twin Telepathy's beam. As a player, I want a cord that latches onto the toughest enemy and drains it to heal me, and hurts anything crossing the cord, so that I feed off big targets. Reactions: Hemorrhage, Cardiac Arrest with Static.
7. **Period Drama** (Blood, Monthly flood, Problem Child, around you). Modify Waters Breaking's timing. As a player, I want a heavy cramp-and-flood every 28 seconds that makes everything nearby bleed hard, so that one huge, predictable moment wrecks a crowd. Reactions: Hemorrhage, Crime Scene with Water.
8. **Fork in the Socket** (Static, Held beam, Bright Spark, NEAREST). Eliminate Static Cling's chain. As a player, I want a jittery beam that locks onto one enemy and grows stronger the longer I hold it, so that I can fry a single big target. Reactions: Overload, Battery with Acid.
9. **Wet Kiss** (Cooties, Homing, Favourite, WEAKEST). Substitute Seeker Siblings' shots with Auntie's lipstick kisses. As a player, I want sloppy homing kisses that leave Cooties lip prints, so that the weakest enemies catch something nasty. Reactions: Patient Zero with Soul, Dirty Needle with Blood.
10. **Runaway Pram** (Force, Bowler, Firstborn, FURTHEST). Adapt Yo-Yo Diet. As a player, I want a pram that rolls out, scoops up enemies and dumps them in a pile at the end, so that a whole line is bowled over. No status (Force), but the pile feeds area weapons.

### Delight 7
1. **Runny Nose** (Mucus, Drip trail, Quiet One, behind you). Adapt Skid Mark. As a player, I want a snot trail dripping behind me that sticks anything crossing it, so that chasers get glued to my path. Reactions: Snail Trail with Oil, Snot Slick with Water.
2. **Lemon Squeezy** (Acid, Marksman, Favourite, NEAREST). Modify Spitball. As a player, I want citrus squirts in the eye that make enemies fire off-target for a moment, so that I get damage and fewer bullets coming at me. Reactions: Acid Rain with Water, Soul Rot with Soul.
3. **Baby Shower** (Water, Rain, Designer Baby, DENSEST CLUSTER). Combine Morning Sickness and party gifts. As a player, I want gifts and confetti to rain down on the biggest crowd, soaking everything, so that one present becomes a shower of damage. Reactions: Public Pool with Cooties, Don't Mix with Oil.
4. **Cod Liver Oil** (Oil, Spoon catapult, Chonker, NEAREST). Put to another use. As a player, I want a spoon that flicks cod liver oil at enemies and makes them gag (they stop shooting for a moment), so that medicine nobody wants works as crowd control. Reactions: Gas Station Sushi with Cooties, Blood Clot with Blood.
5. **Sardine Tin** (Oil, Shotgun, Good Eater, NEAREST). Substitute Shotgun Wedding's pellets. As a player, I want to peel a tin that fires oily homing sardines in a spray, so that a close blast also lubricates the crowd. Reactions: Slip 'n Slide, Vinaigrette with Acid.
6. **Lava Lamp** (Oil, Blob orbit, Bright Spark, around you). Rearrange Premature Evangelation. As a player, I want slow blobs of oil that rise and fall around me, merging and splitting, so that I get a hypnotic shield that sets up Grease Fire. Reactions: Grease Fire with Static, Last Rites with Soul.
7. **Wool Jumper** (Static, Charge aura, Problem Child, around you). Combine Static Cling and Thorny Onesie. As a player, I want an itchy jumper that builds charge as I swim and zaps whatever touches me, so that keeping on the move pays off. Reactions: Overcharged, Poltergeist with Soul.
8. **Mosquito** (Blood, Swarm, Good Eater, NEAREST). Adapt Seeker Siblings. As a player, I want mosquitoes that bite enemies and fly back with blood to heal me, so that my swarm keeps me alive. Reactions: Dirty Needle with Cooties, Blood Pact with Soul.
9. **Nit Comb** (Cooties, Rake, Quiet One, DENSEST CLUSTER). Modify Flagellum Flail. As a player, I want to rake a comb through the crowd, flicking nits that jump between enemies, so that Cooties spread through the pack. Reactions: Snot Rocket with Mucus, The Shakes with Static.

### Delight 6
1. **Sneeze Guard** (Mucus, Wall, Chonker, in front of you). Combine Latex Barrier (Feat) and Mucus. As a player, I want a sheet of snot in front of me that soaks up bullets and slows enemies pushing through, so that I have a weapon that is also cover. Reactions: Ectoplasm with Soul, Scab with Blood.
2. **Sour Patch** (Acid, Caltrops, Good Eater, behind you). Adapt Nappy Mines. As a player, I want sour sweets scattered behind me that fizz when stepped on, so that chasers keep melting. Reactions: Meltdown, Flare-Up with Oil.
3. **Wet Wipe** (Water, Sweeper, Designer Baby, DENSEST CLUSTER). Eliminate Placental Siphon's catch. As a player, I want a wipe that sweeps across the crowd, soaking enemies and wiping off their shields and buffs, so that tough enemies lose their tricks. Reactions: Holy Water with Soul, Diluted in acid.
4. **Heel Prick** (Blood, Needle, Designer Baby, HIGHEST HEALTH). Modify Gene Gun. As a player, I want tiny fast needles that make the toughest enemy bleed and mark a weak spot that crits, so that I can open up big targets. Reactions: Ulcer with Acid, Cardiac Arrest with Static.
5. **Night Light** (Static, Plug-in turret, Bright Spark, NEAREST). Put Baby Monitor (Feat) to another use. As a player, I want to plug in night lights as I swim that zap anything that comes near, so that I leave a glowing trail of guards. Reactions: Live Wire, Short Circuit with Mucus.
6. **Rattle** (Force, Shake wave, Twins, around you). Adapt Thorny Onesie. As a player, I want to shake a rattle whose shockwave gets bigger the more I have been moving, so that I am rewarded for never stopping.

### Delight 5
1. **Water Pistol** (Water, Rapid fire, Firstborn, NEAREST). Substitute Spitball's damage type. As a player, I want a fast, weak squirt gun that soaks everything, so that I set up Water reactions for my other weapons. Reactions: Toaster in the Bath with Static, Acid Rain with Acid.
