DIRECTIVE

Autorun. Autogun. You write the orders.

You don't aim. Your ship runs and shoots by itself. You program it.

Targeting directives: tap a weapon slot to cycle its directive (Nearest, Strongest, Weakest, Lowest Health, Highest Armour, Fastest, Densest Cluster, Elites, Shooters, and more). Pause for the full editor.

Autorun directive: the RUN button switches movement: Kite, Collect, Orbit, Hunt, Hold. Drag anywhere to steer manually.

Loot boxes: every level up opens a box. Pick 1 of 3: new weapons, spells, upgrades or power-ups. Up to 3 weapons and 2 spells.

Fusion: get two compatible weapons to Lv 4 and a fusion card appears, merging them into a legendary weapon and freeing a slot.

Reactions: mix elements. Fire on frozen = Thermal Shock. Shock on poison = Toxic Arc. Arcane on anything = Resonance. Own 2 of an element for a synergy bonus.

Armour reduces every hit. Use Railguns, shred and damage-over-time on armoured foes.

A boss arrives every 3 minutes. Survive.
