Autorun. Autogun. Broadcast live to an audience that is mostly hoping you explode.

You don't aim. Your ship runs and shoots by itself. You program it.

Armoury: tap any weapon slot to open the Armoury: stats, modifiers, fusion progress, and each weapon's targeting directive (Nearest, Strongest, Lowest Health, Highest Armour, Densest Cluster, Revenge and more). Recycle weapons you've outgrown for rerolls.

Autorun directive: the RUN button switches movement: Kite, Collect, Orbit, Hunt, Hold, Guard. Drag anywhere to steer manually.

Loot boxes: every level up opens a box. Pick 1 of 3: new weapons, spells, upgrades or power-ups. Up to 3 weapons and 2 spells.

Fusion: get two compatible weapons to Lv 4 and a fusion card appears, merging them into a legendary weapon and freeing a slot.

Reactions: mix elements. Fire on frozen = Thermal Shock. Shock on poison = Toxic Arc. Arcane on anything = Resonance. Own 2 of an element for a synergy bonus.

Armour reduces every hit. Use Railguns, shred and damage-over-time on armoured foes.

Chrono Anchor: the crystal at the centre of the arena. Stand in its sanctuary to heal.

Rewind: jump 4 seconds back in time. Your future self stays behind as a Paradox Echo that walks the erased timeline backwards, firing your weapons, then collapses in a blast. With a charge ready, death rewinds automatically.

The Show: you are a contestant. The System narrates your every mistake, hands out achievements (sometimes with real rewards), and sponsors send gifts when your viewer count hits milestones. Loot boxes come in Bronze, Silver, Gold and Legendary. Watch out for cursed cards.

Modifiers: loot cards that install into one weapon (3 slots each): Seeking, Splitting, Orbiting, Growing, Boomerang, Ricochet, Freezing, Exploding, Mind Control, Element Swap, Shrapnel. Pick one you already have to power it up.

Enemies: fewer of them, but every one is big, tough and worth a lot of XP.

A boss arrives every 3 minutes. Survive. Or don't. Both are good television.
