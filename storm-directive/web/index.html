One egg. Four hundred million rival swimmers. Five of them are armed. Grow strong enough to break in and be born first.

You are an alien sperm. So is everyone else, plus the host's entire immune system. There is one egg, in the middle of the arena. Grow to level 60, then break its membrane to be born and win.

Weapon trees: at Lv 3, 5, 8 and 10 each weapon branches: pick one of three perks. See the whole tree in the Armoury. You get a box every level, and Fan Boxes (Gold+) drop from elites.

Amoebas eat other monsters and grow. Kill them early, or kill them big for a box.

Swimming takes skill: you turn slowly at speed and drift in turns. Traction upgrades (Sticky Cilia, Hydrodynamic Head) fix that. Shots bounce off cartilage, mitochondria absorb them and burst, acid burns, cilia shove, currents carry and slicks make you slide.

Rival champions (Big Steve, Chad Flagellum and friends) are growing elsewhere on the map. They steal your kills, pick fights when they feel big, and run when they don't. If one reaches level 60 first it swims to the egg and starts breaking in: stop it, or you lose. Kill one for a Fan Box and a level's worth of XP. The race board under the minimap shows who is ahead.

You don't aim. You swim and shoot by yourself. You program the directives.

Armoury: tap any weapon slot: stats, modifiers, fusion progress, and each weapon's targeting directive (Nearest, Strongest, Lowest Health, Highest Armour, Densest Cluster, Revenge and more). Recycle weapons you've outgrown for rerolls.

Weapon slots: you start with 3 and grow a new one at levels 15, 30 and 45 (6 in total). Each new slot comes with a box of new weapons.

Autorun directive: the RUN button switches movement: Kite, Collect, Orbit, Hunt, Hold, Nest. Drag anywhere to steer manually.

Loot boxes: every level up opens one. Pick 1 of 3: weapons, spells, upgrades, modifiers or power-ups. Bronze, Silver, Gold, Legendary. Watch out for cursed cards.

Modifiers: install into one weapon (3 slots each): Seeking, Splitting, Orbiting, Growing, Boomerang, Ricochet, Freezing, Exploding, Mind Control, Element Swap, Shrapnel.

Fusion: two compatible weapons at Lv 4 fuse into a legendary one and free a slot.

Reactions: mix elements. Fire on frozen = Thermal Shock. Shock on poison = Toxic Arc. Arcane on anything = Resonance.

Rewind: jump 4 seconds back. Your future self stays behind as a Paradox Echo that fights for you, then pops. With a charge ready, death rewinds automatically.

Deep trees: each weapon now branches at Lv 3, 5, 8 and 10 (mastery), with three perks to choose from each time. Weapons go to Lv 10.

Modifier Forge: five new modifiers (Chaining, Pulsing, Magnetic, Delayed, Mirror). Certain pairs on one weapon form Duo Combos, listed in the Armoury.

Zoom: pinch with two fingers to change magnification (24x to 80x).

Mutations: your sperm upgrades change your body: Split Personality grows extra tails, Thick Skin and Shell Suit a bigger plated head, Sticky Cilia grows cilia, element upgrades dye your midpiece.

Gene Bank: every run banks DNA. Spend it on inherited traits, extra starter weapons and tag dyes.

Settings: darkfield microscope, high specimen detail, clean clinical view, minimal HUD, CASA Pro panel (live motility stats), landscape layout and four narrators.

The egg's glow heals you. A boss arrives every 3 minutes. From 15:00 the host's immune system surges. Swim fast.
