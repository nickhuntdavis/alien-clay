# Feat ideas

Source: v9.19 guide. Damage types: Force, Acid, Mucus, Static, Cooties, Oil, Water, Blood, Soul.
Delight (1 to 10) means joke, visual surprise and "I'd show a friend", not power. Scores are a first-pass judgement.

## 1. Current Feats (18), as user stories

Ranked by usefulness within each group (damage type, cooldown).

### Damage, general
1. **Stork Drop** (Acid, 5s): As a player, I want a stork to drop something heavy on my target and leave acid on the ground, so that I get a big single hit plus lasting area damage.
2. **Brainstorm** (Static, 6s): As a player, I want static to strike 5 targets at once, so that I can thin out a crowd without aiming.
3. **Running With Scissors** (Force, 6s): As a player, I want a ring of 16 blades to fly outward, so that I can hit everything around me at once.
4. **Power Shower** (Water, 7s): As a player, I want a soapy blast around me that erases enemy bullets, so that I get damage and breathing room in one press.
5. **Dummy Spit** (Acid, 8s): As a player, I want to fling a dummy at the toughest enemy that bursts into acid when it dies, so that big threats punish themselves.
6. **Tantrum** (Static, 9s): As a player, I want a blast that grows the more I have been hurt lately, so that taking a beating turns into a payoff.

### Area and crowd control
1. **Sofa Crevice** (Soul, 10s): As a player, I want a singularity that drags and crushes, so that I can bunch enemies up and grind them down.
2. **Naughty Corner** (Water, 9s): As a player, I want a ring on the floor that roots anything inside while bullets pass through, so that I can pin enemies without blocking my own shots.
3. **Dutch Oven** (Cooties, 9s): As a player, I want a drifting cloud of stacking Cooties fumes, so that enemies sicken the longer they stay in it.
4. **Baby Monitor** (Static, 13s): As a player, I want a turret that watches and shoots for 10 seconds, so that I get extra firepower while I keep moving.

### Defence and healing
1. **Latex Barrier** (Blood, 12s): As a player, I want a stretchy shield that bounces enemies off and flings bullets back, so that I can block a bad moment and punish it.
2. **Kiss It Better** (Cooties, 14s): As a player, I want to restore 15% of my health, so that I can recover mid-run without a pickup.
3. **Nap Time** (Soul, 16s): As a player, I want every enemy and bullet slowed to a crawl, so that I can escape or reposition when overwhelmed.
4. **Out of Body** (Soul, 13s): As a player, I want to slip out of my body, untouchable and faster, damaging what I pass through, so that I can dodge a lethal moment and deal damage while doing it.

### Character-locked
1. **Lightbulb Moment** (Static, 7s): As a Bright Spark player, I want a bolt that jumps through up to 6 enemies, so that my Feat fits my shocking character.
2. **Elbows Out** (Force, 8s): As a Firstborn player, I want to hit and shove back everything close to me, so that I can clear space the way an eldest child would.
3. **Come Play With Us** (Force, 9s): As a Twins player, I want me and my twin to reach out together and pull enemies in close, so that the pair's Feat uses both of us.
4. **Belly Flop** (Force, 10s): As a Chonker player, I want to land on the thickest crowd nearby, so that my size becomes a weapon.

Current spread: Force 4, Static 4, Soul 3, Acid 2, Cooties 2, Water 2, Blood 1, Mucus 0, Oil 0.

## 2. New Feat ideas (33), ranked by delight

Each line: name (damage type, cooldown), SCAMPER move, user story, reactions it sets up.

### Delight 9
1. **Pass the Parcel** (Force, 10s). Adapt Dummy Spit. As a player, I want a parcel with three layers that explodes three times in a row, so that one throw becomes a party game of damage.
2. **Chickenpox Party** (Cooties, 9s). Combine Dummy Spit and Cooties. As a player, I want spots to land on 3 enemies that each spread on death, so that a few hits can infect a whole crowd. Reactions: Pandemic, The Shakes.
3. **Nappy Leak** (Oil, 10s). Adapt. As a player, I want to lay an oil trail behind me, so that I swim faster (Lubed Up) while chasers slide about. Reactions: Blood Clot with Blood, Slip 'n Slide.

### Delight 8
1. **Bogey Flick** (Mucus, 5s). Reverse Stork Drop. As a player, I want to flick a bogey at the toughest enemy that sticks and bursts shots back out when it dies, so that big targets punish themselves. Reactions: Booger, Scab.
2. **Snail Mail** (Mucus, 8s). Adapt Running With Scissors. As a player, I want a ring of slow snot slugs to crawl outward and leave slowing trails, so that I control the space around me. Reactions: Snail Trail with Oil, Snot Slick with Water.
3. **Dirty Nappy** (Cooties, 7s). Substitute Stork Drop. As a player, I want the stork to drop a nappy that splats into a Cooties pool, so that I get an area hit that sickens. Reactions: Gas Station Sushi with Oil, Penicillin with Acid.
4. **Haunted Pram** (Soul, 13s). Modify Baby Monitor. As a player, I want a spectral pram to circle me and shoot, so that a ghost guards me for a while. Reaction: Baptism.
5. **Nose Blow** (Mucus, 7s). Substitute Brainstorm. As a player, I want to blow snot globs onto several targets at once, so that enemies get snotty, slow and stuck. Reactions: Short Circuit with Static, Heartburn with Acid.
6. **Chip Pan** (Oil, 8s). Combine Stork Drop and a slick. As a player, I want to drop a chip pan that leaves an oil slick, so that knockback and reactions on those enemies get stronger. Reactions: Grease Fire with Static, Vinaigrette with Acid.
7. **Puddle Jumper** (Water, 9s). Adapt Belly Flop. As a player, I want a splashing dash that leaves a puddle trail and rinses debuffs off me, so that I can reposition and clean up in one move. Reactions: Rinse, Public Pool.
8. **Night Terror** (Soul, 11s). Magnify Nap Time. As a player, I want enemies to flee in fear for 3 seconds, so that I get space without slowing the whole screen. Reaction: Full Possession.
9. **Doorknob** (Static, 6s). Adapt Tantrum. As a player, I want to charge up so that the next enemy to touch me takes a triple-damage zap, so that being touched becomes the punishment. Reactions: Overload, Live Wire.

### Delight 7
1. **Blood Donor** (Blood, 11s). Reverse Kiss It Better. As a player, I want to heal for each bleeding enemy on screen, so that bleeding them pays me back. Reactions: Top-Up, Hemorrhage.
2. **Ouija Board** (Soul, 12s). Adapt Naughty Corner. As a player, I want a board on the floor that curses everything inside, so that enemies sometimes turn on their mates. Reactions: Holy Water, Ectoplasm.
3. **Baby Oil** (Oil, 11s). Modify Kiss It Better. As a player, I want to coat myself for 3 seconds (faster, glancing hits, contact lubricates enemies), so that I can slip through a crowd. Reactions: Lubed Up, Last Rites.
4. **Wet Floor Sign** (Oil, 12s). Eliminate Naughty Corner's root. As a player, I want a slick ring where everything inside slides out to the edge, so that I can push a crowd away without rooting it. Reactions: Don't Mix with Water, Slip 'n Slide.
5. **Bath Bomb** (Water, 9s). Modify Stork Drop. As a player, I want a fizzing bomb that soaks the target and splashes its statuses onto neighbours, so that one afflicted enemy spreads its status to the rest. Reactions: Toaster in the Bath, Acid Rain.
6. **Coughs and Sneezes** (Cooties, 8s). Combine Power Shower and Cooties. As a player, I want a cone sneeze that gives two doses of Cooties, so that a crowd gets sick fast. Reactions: Pandemic, Snot Rocket with Mucus, Dirty Needle with Blood.
7. **Balloon Rub** (Static, 8s). Combine Elbows Out and Static. As a player, I want a balloon rubbed on hair that pulls enemies in a little and shocks them, so that I get a small pull and a zap. Reactions: Overcharged, Poltergeist.
8. **Scab Picker** (Blood, 9s). Adapt Tantrum. As a player, I want to pop every bleeding enemy at once for its remaining bleed, so that I cash in all my bleeding in one press. Reactions: Cardiac Arrest, Hemorrhage.
9. **Grazed Knee** (Blood, 8s). Adapt Belly Flop. As a player, I want to skid along the floor and leave a blood streak that bleeds anything crossing it, so that a dash becomes a trap. Reactions: Crime Scene, Cardiac Arrest.
10. **Sprinkler** (Water, 12s). Put Baby Monitor to another use. As a player, I want a turret that sprays water and splashes statuses between enemies, so that my reactions spread without me. Reactions: Toaster in the Bath, Acid Rain.

### Delight 6
1. **Cling Film** (Mucus, 12s). Combine Latex Barrier and Mucus. As a player, I want a sticky wrap that enemies stick to and bullets sink into, so that I have a Mucus defence that slows what touches it. Reactions: Snot Shield, Ectoplasm.
2. **Imaginary Friend** (Soul, 14s). Eliminate Out of Body's dodge. As a player, I want a ghost double of me to fire my weapons for 5 seconds, so that I get extra damage without moving. Overlaps Inheritance and the Twins.
3. **Slip Hazard** (Oil, 7s). Put Baby Monitor to another use. As a player, I want a turret that sprays oil slicks around itself, so that the area around it becomes a reaction zone. Reactions: Grease Fire with Static, Vinaigrette with Acid.
4. **Tummy Time** (Force, 8s). Reverse Belly Flop. As a player, I want everything near me knocked flat and shoved away, so that I can break a crowd off me.
5. **Nosebleed** (Blood, 10s). Reverse Kiss It Better. As a player, I want to spend 8% HP to make everything on screen bleed and leave a healing pool, so that I trade health for damage and win it back. Reactions: Top-Up, Crime Scene with Water.
6. **Paper Cut** (Blood, 6s). Modify Running With Scissors. As a player, I want thin, fast blades that make enemies bleed, so that moving crowds take stacking damage. Reaction: Cardiac Arrest with Static, plus the Hemorrhage count.
7. **Glue Stick** (Mucus, 9s). Reverse Sofa Crevice. As a player, I want a sticky field that holds enemies in place instead of dragging them, so that I can pin a crowd. Reactions: Short Circuit, Heartburn.
8. **Sherbet Dip** (Acid, 6s). Modify Brainstorm. As a player, I want fizzing acid pops on 4 targets that strip armour, so that armoured enemies stop shrugging me off. Reaction: Meltdown.

### Delight 5
1. **Jump Leads** (Static, 12s). Put to another use. As a player, I want my Static hits to count double and arc further for 4 seconds, so that I get a short burst window for Static builds. Reactions: Battery with Acid, Overload.
2. **Reflux** (Acid, 7s). Adapt Power Shower. As a player, I want an acid burp in a cone that melts armour, so that I can open up armoured enemies in front of me. Reactions: Ulcer, Shell Shock.
3. **Hose Down** (Water, 10s). Eliminate Power Shower's circle. As a player, I want a piercing jet in a line with big knockback, so that I can push a column of enemies back. Reactions: Holy Water, Waterlogged.
