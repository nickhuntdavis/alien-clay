# Feat ideas

Source: v9.20 guide. Damage types: Force, Acid, Mucus, Static, Cooties, Oil, Water, Blood, Soul.
Second pass: every idea from the first list was run back through SCAMPER (Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify/Magnify, Put to another use, Eliminate, Reverse) to find the funnier, more visual version. Weak or overlapping ideas were cut or merged (see section 5).

**Delight (1 to 10)** means joke, visual surprise and "I'd show a friend", not power.
**Cost**: attacking Feats spend stamina (marked *stamina*); utility Feats keep a cooldown (marked with seconds).
**On the slide** follows `STYLE.md`: things sit in the fluid, soft edges, organic shapes, motion over colour, readable in Darkfield. Jokey props (a parcel, a duck, a sign) may stay cartoon-like: the joke wins.

## 1. The headline five

These are the ones to build first. Each one has a moment a player will screenshot, and each fills a gap (decoys, traps, a fear effect, Oil and Mucus Feats, an audio gag).

### 1. Pressie (Force, stamina) · Delight 10
> *"Whoever's holding it when the music stops. House rules."*
- **The moment:** the music cuts out mid-fight, the slide goes quiet for half a beat, and the last enemy holding the parcel explodes in a shower of wrapping paper.
- **What it does:** a lumpy parcel lands on an enemy. Every 0.6s it is *passed* to the nearest other enemy and a layer tears off in a small Force burst. After 3 to 5 passes (random) the music stops and the final holder takes a big hit of a **random damage type** (the gift), which can set off a reaction with whatever is already on it.
- **On the slide:** a wobbly bundle of translucent tissue-paper membranes, each layer a slightly different grey, crinkling as it hops. Torn layers drift away like shed skin. Darkfield: bright paper rims on black.
- **SCAMPER:** Adapt Dummy Spit (sticks to a target), Combine with the game's own music (the audio dip is the tell), Modify the payload into a lucky dip.
- **Reactions:** any; the random filling makes it a reaction lottery.
- **Lv 4 paths:** **Too Much Tape**: two more layers, so two more passes. / **Sweets in Every Layer**: every layer drops a random damage type, not just the last.

### 2. Monster Under the Bed (Soul, stamina) · Delight 10
> *"It was there. You SAW it."*
- **The moment:** the biggest thing on screen is grabbed by something you never quite see and dragged down into a hole in the slide. Three seconds later it is spat back out, cursed and fighting for you.
- **What it does:** a shadow opens under the toughest non-boss enemy nearby. It is pulled under and out of play for 3s, then thrown back up cursed (counts as six Soul hits, so it lands in Full Possession). Enemies near the hole flee for 2s. Bosses are spooked and take a big hit instead.
- **On the slide:** a soft dark pool, feathered edge, no ring; a few cartoon claw tips curl over the rim, then the enemy sinks with a wobble. Darkfield: the pool is *blacker than black* (a faint inverted glow at the edge so it reads).
- **SCAMPER:** Magnify Nap Time and Night Terror (fear instead of slow), Reverse Sofa Crevice (one thing swallowed, not a crowd dragged).
- **Reactions:** Full Possession, Ectoplasm (Mucus), Last Rites (Oil on the returner).
- **Lv 4 paths:** **Two Monsters**: a second hole under the next-toughest enemy. / **It Kept One**: 25% chance the enemy never comes back (not elites).

### 3. Ouija Board (Soul, stamina) · Delight 10
> *"Is anybody there? Yes. It's rude."*
- **The moment:** a planchette slides across the screen spelling **B-U-M**, letter by letter, and every enemy it passes under turns on its mates.
- **What it does:** a board lays itself under the crowd. A planchette glides through 3 to 5 letters (picked from a short word list: BUM, WEE, POO, NUMPTY, PLONKER, WILLY, TROUT), each letter a stop that curses everything near it. The finished word flashes in the floater text.
- **On the slide:** no board outline (zones have no borders): just faded letters floating in the fluid like stained cells, and a cartoon planchette prop with a soft glow. Soul passes through walls, so in campaign levels it spells through the maze.
- **SCAMPER:** Adapt Naughty Corner (a floor zone), Modify the shape into a path, Put to another use (the word list is a joke delivery system we can keep adding to).
- **Reactions:** Holy Water (Water), Ectoplasm (Mucus), Patient Zero (Cooties), Blood Pact (Blood).
- **Lv 4 paths:** **Long Word**: always picks a 6+ letter word. / **Goodbye**: when the word ends, every cursed enemy takes a hit for each letter.

### 4. Rubber Duck (Water, 11s) · Delight 10
> *"Squeak."*
- **The moment:** the whole screen turns to chase a rubber duck. It squeaks every time something bites it, and when it pops it soaks everyone who was mobbing it.
- **What it does:** drops a decoy duck that bobs on the current. Enemies target it, and enemy bullets aim at it, for 4s. Every hit on it squeaks and splashes Water on the hitter. When it pops (time out or broken), a soak burst hits everything nearby.
- **On the slide:** the one bright yellow cartoon prop allowed (it is a joke), bobbing with the flow, leaving tiny ripples. In Lab view it is a pale greyscale duck that still bobs.
- **SCAMPER:** Substitute Baby Monitor's turret with a decoy, Reverse its job (it attracts fire instead of firing). Decoys are a role no Feat fills yet.
- **Reactions:** Toaster in the Bath (Static), Public Pool (Cooties), Holy Water (Soul), Crime Scene (Blood). Soaking a whole mob makes the next Static hit huge.
- **Lv 4 paths:** **Ducklings**: three small ducks instead of one. / **Bath Time**: the pop leaves a puddle that rinses you (Rinse) and soaks anything that wades in.

### 5. Chip Pan Fire (Oil, stamina) · Delight 10
> *"Never throw water on it. Never."*
- **The moment:** a chip pan lands in the crowd, sizzles, and then goes up. If you hit it with Water first, it goes up *much* worse.
- **What it does:** drops a chip pan that leaves an Oil slick (lubricating everything in it). After 2s it catches fire on its own (Grease Fire patch). Any Water hit on it before then makes it erupt in a Don't Mix fireball that flings the whole crowd apart and lights the slick.
- **On the slide:** a cartoon pan prop with a lilac oil sheen spreading as a soft blob (the slick look we already have), bubbles popping faster as it nears 2s, then a feathered orange-white flare drawn with `'lighter'` so it shows in both fields.
- **SCAMPER:** Combine Stork Drop with a slick, Adapt the 1980s public information film (the joke is the warning), Reverse the usual reaction (Water makes it worse, not better).
- **Reactions:** Grease Fire, Don't Mix, Napalm (with Acid and Static), Flare-Up (in an acid pit), Vinaigrette (Acid).
- **Lv 4 paths:** **Deep Fat Fryer**: the slick is twice as wide. / **Damp Tea Towel**: if you perform it again while the pan is still burning, the fire goes out in a stun burst.

## 2. Delight 9

### 6. Mum's Hanky (Mucus, 12s)
> *"Hold still. Hold STILL."*
- **What it does:** a giant hanky comes down and scrubs you: clears every debuff on you and heals 5%. Then it is flicked out in a wide snap that makes everything nearby snotty and shoves it back.
- **On the slide:** a soft cloth shape (cartoon prop) wipes across you; you wobble side to side under it. The flick leaves strings of snot in the fluid.
- **SCAMPER:** Substitute Kiss It Better's heal with a cleanse, Combine with Elbows Out's shove, Adapt every British childhood.
- **Reactions:** Snot Shield (you stand in the leftover blob), Ectoplasm, Short Circuit.
- **Lv 4 paths:** **Licked It First**: the snap also gives one dose of Cooties. / **Spit and Polish**: 1.5s where nothing can touch you.

### 7. Someone Get the Car (Water, stamina)
> *"Your waters have broken. So has everyone else's day."*
- **What it does:** a huge wave gushes out from you to the edge of the screen. It soaks everything, sweeps enemies and their bullets outward, and leaves you in clear water for a moment.
- **On the slide:** a ring of foam and bubbles racing outward, with a frothy leading lip and droplets thrown ahead. Enemies tumble as it passes. Not a hard ring: the foam density falls off behind it.
- **SCAMPER:** Magnify Power Shower to full screen, Adapt the biggest pregnancy joke we have.
- **Reactions:** Toaster in the Bath (one Static hit after this zaps the whole screen), Public Pool, Crime Scene.
- **Lv 4 paths:** **False Alarm**: costs half the stamina, half the reach. / **Twins**: a second wave 1s later.

### 8. Pull My Finger (Cooties, stamina)
> *"Go on. Pull it."*
- **What it does:** a little finger pokes out of you. The first enemy to touch it sets off a cone of fumes behind you that gives two doses of Cooties to everything in it. If nothing touches it in 4s, you get bored and pull it yourself.
- **On the slide:** a small wiggling pseudopod; the blast is a soft brown-grey cloud that bellows outward and drifts. One loud comedy sound.
- **SCAMPER:** Reverse Dutch Oven (a trap they trigger, not a cloud you leave), Adapt the dad joke.
- **Reactions:** Pandemic, The Shakes (Static), Gas Station Sushi (Oil), Snot Rocket (Mucus).
- **Lv 4 paths:** **Both Hands**: two fingers, two clouds. / **Follow-Through**: the cloud stays as a Dutch Oven for 3s.

### 9. Snot Bubble (Mucus, 13s)
> *"You've got a little something."*
- **What it does:** a snot bubble swells from your nose until you are inside it. Enemies that touch it stick to the outside; bullets sink in. After 3s it pops and sprays every stuck enemy and swallowed bullet outward as snot.
- **On the slide:** a wobbly translucent bubble with a bright thin membrane rim (Darkfield reference look), breathing in and out, enemies glued to its skin. A whistling inhale sound as it grows.
- **SCAMPER:** Combine Latex Barrier with Mucus (replaces Cling Film), Magnify the pop.
- **Reactions:** Booger (stuck enemies take the six hits fast), Scab (Blood), Snot Slick (Water).
- **Lv 4 paths:** **Big Sniff**: it grows 50% bigger. / **Sneeze It Out**: the pop throws stuck enemies like cannonballs.

### 10. Sleeping Lions (Static, stamina)
> *"Anyone who moves is OUT."*
- **What it does:** the music stops and every enemy on screen freezes, stunned for 1.5s. Anything that moves during it (bosses, enemies that resist stuns) gets "OUT!" and takes a big Static hit.
- **On the slide:** everything stops, including drifting particles; only you move. A faint crackle of static runs over the frozen bodies. "OUT!" floats over each victim.
- **SCAMPER:** Combine Nap Time with a party game, Eliminate the slow (a full stop instead), Reverse the target (it punishes the things that ignore it).
- **Reactions:** Overload, Toaster in the Bath, Cardiac Arrest (Blood).
- **Lv 4 paths:** **Lie Very Still**: the freeze lasts 2.5s. / **Sore Loser**: bosses that are called OUT take triple.

### 11. Fortune Teller (Cooties, stamina)
> *"Pick a number. Pick a colour. Lift the flap."*
- **What it does:** a paper fortune teller snaps open and shut over the nearest crowd four times. Each snap picks one of four fortunes: two doses of Cooties, a heal for you, a stun, or a big hit. The last flap is always a fortune that matches what is already on them (a reaction if one exists).
- **On the slide:** a four-petal folded shape (cartoon prop) that opens and closes like a mouth, with a soft shadow on the fluid. The chosen fortune shows as one word ("POORLY", "LUCKY", "STUCK", "BOOM").
- **SCAMPER:** Substitute Stork Drop's single hit with a lucky dip, Adapt the playground toy.
- **Reactions:** whichever the last flap finds.
- **Lv 4 paths:** **Crush on You**: every fortune also heals you 2%. / **Cheating**: no healing fortune, but the big hit is twice as big.

### 12. Chickenpox Party (Cooties, stamina)
> *"Better to get it over with while they're young."*
- **What it does:** spots land on 3 enemies. Spotty enemies are drawn to each other (everyone wants to be at the party), bunch up, and spread spots on contact. When one dies, its spots jump to every enemy touching it.
- **On the slide:** small bright dots appearing on the enemy bodies (motion: they pulse), and infected enemies drifting into a clump.
- **SCAMPER:** Combine Dummy Spit with Cooties, Magnify with the party pull (a crowd gatherer that sets up area Feats).
- **Reactions:** Pandemic, The Shakes, Flu Season (Mucus, Cooties and Water).
- **Lv 4 paths:** **Party Bags**: each dying guest drops a small stamina blob. / **Itchy**: spotty enemies take 50% more from everything.

### 13. Projectile Vomit (Acid, stamina)
> *"From one end of the room to the other. Impressive, really."*
- **What it does:** a long cone of acid sick that melts armour and knocks you backwards from the recoil (a free little dodge).
- **On the slide:** a feathered jet of acid green with fizz bubbles along its length, spattering into small pools where it lands. You lurch back.
- **SCAMPER:** Magnify Reflux, Combine with movement (recoil).
- **Reactions:** Heartburn (Mucus), Ulcer (Blood), Vinaigrette (Oil), Meltdown, Shell Shock.
- **Lv 4 paths:** **Second Helping**: a second, shorter cone 0.5s later. / **Carrots**: leaves chunky pools that keep melting for 3s.

## 3. Delight 8

### 14. Greased Piglet (Oil, 11s)
*"Catch it if you can."* You are coated for 4s: faster, enemies that touch you slide off and are flung (Slip 'n Slide), and every contact lubricates them. **On the slide:** a lilac sheen on your body with beads running off your tail. **SCAMPER:** Modify Baby Oil, Adapt the village-fair game. **Reactions:** Lubed Up, Slip 'n Slide, Last Rites. **Paths:** **Squeal** (flung enemies hurt what they hit) / **Lard** (lasts twice as long).

### 15. Runaway Pram (Soul, stamina)
*"Mind the steps."* A ghostly pram rattles in a straight line from you, through walls (Soul), bowling through the crowd and cursing what it hits; a ghost baby inside throws rattles. **On the slide:** a translucent pram prop with a soft trail, wheels wobbling. **SCAMPER:** Modify Baby Monitor (moving, not standing), Adapt the film-steps scene. **Reactions:** Holy Water, Last Rites, Full Possession. **Paths:** **Pushchair Twins** (two prams, angled) / **No Brakes** (bounces off the screen edge twice).

### 16. Snail Mail (Mucus, stamina)
*"Second class. It'll get there."* A ring of snot slugs crawls outward, each carrying a tiny envelope; they leave slowing trails and "deliver" (burst) on the first enemy they reach. **On the slide:** soft grey slugs with glistening trails that fade behind them. **SCAMPER:** Adapt Running With Scissors (slow ring instead of fast). **Reactions:** Snail Trail (Oil), Snot Slick (Water), Glued Down. **Paths:** **First Class** (twice as fast) / **Signed For** (each delivery sticks the enemy fast).

### 17. Blood Brothers (Blood, stamina)
*"Spit, shake, swear on it."* Ties 4 pairs of nearby enemies with a thin red thread for 5s; half of any damage one takes is copied to its partner, and both bleed. **On the slide:** fine wavering red threads between pairs that thicken when damage passes. **SCAMPER:** Combine Blood with the Twins, Put Red Tape's bundle idea to Feat use. **Reactions:** Cardiac Arrest (Static), Hemorrhage, Blood Pact (Soul). **Paths:** **Pinky Promise** (full damage copies) / **Family Feud** (tied pairs are pulled into each other).

### 18. Balloon Rub (Static, 12s)
*"Now stick it on the wall."* You rub a balloon on your head; it floats beside you for 4s and pulls enemy bullets into it. Each caught bullet charges it, and when it pops it sends a Static arc per bullet. **On the slide:** a soft round balloon with a static shimmer; bullets curve towards it and stick. **SCAMPER:** Put Latex Barrier to another use (bullet magnet, not a wall). **Reactions:** Overload, Toaster in the Bath, Poltergeist. **Paths:** **Two Balloons** / **Hair Raiser** (also pulls small enemies).

### 19. Birthday Bumps (Force, stamina)
*"One for luck."* The toughest enemy nearby is thrown up and slammed down once per wave number (cap 8), each slam a Force hit around it. The count floats up ("…6! 7! AND ONE FOR LUCK!"). **On the slide:** the enemy grows and shrinks to show height, a soft shadow on the fluid. **SCAMPER:** Adapt Belly Flop, Magnify with the run's own progress. **Reactions:** none (Force), but slams shove everyone into each other. **Paths:** **Pinch and a Punch** (last slam stuns) / **Late Birthday** (always 8).

### 20. Bundle! (Force, stamina)
*"BUNDLE!"* You shout it, and every enemy nearby piles onto the toughest one. It takes a hit for each enemy in the pile; the pile is stuck for 1.5s. **On the slide:** enemies rushing inward into a heap that wobbles, then sorts itself out. **SCAMPER:** Reverse Come Play With Us (they pile on each other, not you), Combine with a pull. **Reactions:** sets up every area Feat (Dutch Oven, Sofa Crevice, Chip Pan Fire). **Paths:** **Stacks On** (pile lasts 3s) / **Pile Driver** (the pile is slammed for its total).

### 21. Carpet Shuffle (Static, 10s)
*"Don't touch the door handle."* For 4s you build charge with every unit you swim. Then it releases on the nearest enemy (or the next one to touch you) as one zap that scales with distance travelled. **On the slide:** small sparks gather on your tail and brighten as you swim; the release is a jagged white arc. **SCAMPER:** Merge Doorknob and Jump Leads; Put autorun's constant movement to use. **Reactions:** Battery (Acid), Cardiac Arrest, Live Wire (in a current). **Paths:** **Nylon Socks** (charge builds twice as fast) / **Chain Static** (the zap jumps 4 times).

### 22. Period Drama (Blood, stamina) *(tone check before building)*
*"Monthly. Unmissable."* A slow red tide rolls across the screen from one edge, sweeping enemies with it and leaving a Crime Scene pool behind. **On the slide:** a soft wash with a darker, frilled leading edge and drifting clots; never a flat fill. **SCAMPER:** Combine Blood with the Cramp boss's month joke, Magnify Crime Scene. **Reactions:** Crime Scene, Top-Up (you stand in it), Blood Clot (Oil). **Paths:** **Heavy Flow** (wider tide) / **Costume Change** (a second tide from the opposite edge).

### 23. Shaken Fizzy Pop (Acid, stamina)
*"Give it a good shake first."* The longer the Feat sits ready in its slot, the more pressure it builds (up to 6s). When performed, it sprays an acid geyser forward and rockets you backward. A fully shaken bottle triples the damage. **On the slide:** a fizzing column of acid-green bubbles with a foaming tip; bubbles rise inside the Feat slot as it charges. **SCAMPER:** Reverse stamina Feats (waiting is the charge), Combine with recoil movement. **Reactions:** Battery (Static), Acid Rain (Water), Meltdown. **Paths:** **Mint Dropped In** (full pressure in 3s) / **Shared Bottle** (sprays in three directions).
