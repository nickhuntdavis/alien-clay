Discoveries Through Experimentation

🔬

Unexpected Properties

What if we spin it at the right speed?

The Discovery: Like alien metal that glows when spun, AI reveals unexpected capabilities through experimentation - not just as a replacement for existing tools.

The Implication: Your system wasn't designed to harness properties you didn't know existed. Time to redesign.

⚡

Hidden Inefficiencies

Problems you didn't know existed

The Discovery: Your wood-burning system was "stable" - but only because you hadn't discovered a vastly more efficient fuel source.

The Implication: Destabilizing equilibrium reveals inefficiencies invisible in a stable system.

🌊

Cascading Effects

One change ripples through everything

The Discovery: Injecting AI in one place doesn't just add a feature - it transforms the entire system architecture.

The Implication: Think in ecosystems, ripples, and paradigms - not features.

📊

Real World: Travix Qualitative Tagging

One successful experiment

The Experiment: Instead of using AI as a chatbot or interface layer, I used it for narrow, invisible tasks humans couldn't do effectively - analyzing qualitative feedback at scale.

The Discovery: This revealed systemic inefficiencies in how we gathered insights, leading to complete workflow redesign.

The Cascade: What started as "let's try AI for tagging" became "we need to rethink our entire feedback analysis pipeline."

This is one branch. Imagine the unexplored branches.

⚠️

The Substitution Trap

What everyone else is doing

The Trap: "Let's replace our chatbot with an LLM" or "Let's use AI instead of forms."

The Problem: You're asking "Can this replace steel?" instead of "What happens when we spin it?"

The Risk: Safe, predictable, and limiting. You'll never discover the light source.

🔮

The Vision

What we could discover together

The Opportunity: Not just building AI features for Microsoft's clients, but pioneering the discovery of what AI fundamentally is.

The Method: Experimentation, destabilization, and embracing the unknown.

The Question: Are we brave enough to discover what AI really is, not just what it can replace?

About This Exploration

This interactive piece represents a philosophy about AI in product design: it's not about finding problems to solve with AI, but discovering what AI can do that we haven't imagined yet.

Created by Nick Hunt Davis - Science fiction fan, systems thinker, design pioneer.
